Guardrails

It knows a lot. It says very little.

Your AI uses what it knows to understand a call. What it may say depends on who is calling. Those limits are written in code, not left to the AI.

  1. It says it's an AI in its first line, every call.
  2. It never speaks an OTP, card, account, PAN or Aadhaar number, unless you type it in yourself.
  3. It never agrees to a payment or shares a UPI ID.
  4. It never says you're away or the house is empty, except to family.
  5. "Hospital" or "accident" alerts you at once.
  6. You can see and delete everything it remembers.
